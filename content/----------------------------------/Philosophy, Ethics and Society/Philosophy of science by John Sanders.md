[[Philosophy of science by John Sanders]] by [[John Sanders]] (Rochester Institute of Technology) [^1].  
Lectures exploring the logic, structure, and epistemology of scientific inquiry. Sanders emphasizes how science differs from other forms of knowledge through its reliance on empirical testing, falsifiability ([[Karl Popper]]), and the theory-ladenness of observation ([[Thomas Kuhn]], [[Norwood Russell Hanson]]).  

Connects to [[Design epistemology]]: both science and [[Design|design]] rely on iterative testing, model-building, and situated reasoning.  
Also relates to [[Luhmann and systems theory]] — seeing science as a self-correcting system of beliefs guided by evidence and methodological skepticism.
