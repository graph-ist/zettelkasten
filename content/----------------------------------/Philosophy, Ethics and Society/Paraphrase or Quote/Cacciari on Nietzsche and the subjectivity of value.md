According to [[Massimo Cacciari]], all that is evaluated—the [[Value]] we attribute—depends on the [[Subjectivity]] of the evaluator. Every value is subjective by nature; the very definition of value implies dependence on our act of evaluation, and thus lacks intrinsic worth.

We should not assign value to things if we want them to truly have value. Values are what we evaluate, and when we attribute value to something, we destroy it. Cacciari interprets this in the context of [[Friedrich Nietzsche]]’s critique of [[Morality]].
