From _Anti-Oedipus: Capitalism and Schizophrenia_ by [[Gilles Deleuze]] and [[Félix Guattari]]:

*“When a man and a woman decide their association should be legalized with a marriage ceremony, they pose themselves a problem which will continue through the marriage: now that they are married are they staying together because they wish to or because they must?”* (60, p. 119)
