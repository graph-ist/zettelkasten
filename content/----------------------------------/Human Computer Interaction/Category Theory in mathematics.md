[[Category Theory]] is a branch of mathematics that studies the structure of mathematical objects and the relationships between them. Though highly abstract, it has proven to be a powerful tool for examining a wide range of concepts and structures.

In category theory, a category is defined as a collection of objects together with arrows (or _morphisms_) connecting them. These arrows generalize functions and are used to formalize the relations between objects within the category.

An introduction for programmers by Bartosz Milewski is available on youtube [^1] [^2].
