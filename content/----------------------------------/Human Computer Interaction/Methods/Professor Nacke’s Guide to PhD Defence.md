A guide for defending academic thesis by Prof. Nacke [^1].

| **PhD Defence Questions** | **How to Answer** | **Answer Templates** |
|----------------------------|------------------|----------------------|
| **What is the main contribution of your research?** | **Start with the significance, then the specifics.** Briefly state the key implications of your findings for the field, then offer a concise explanation of your main contributions. | *My research offers a critical solution to a long-standing problem in [your field] by establishing that [briefly explain your main findings].* |
| **Can you explain the methodology used in your research?** | **Focus on the why, not just the what.** Briefly explain the methods you used, then emphasize why you chose them and how they were particularly suited to answer your research questions. | *I employed robust research methods, including [mention the research methods used], to ensure the validity and reliability of my findings. These methods were carefully selected because [explain rationale].* |
| **How does your work differ from existing research?** | **Highlight key innovations and unique aspects.** Briefly mention 1–2 key differences from existing research, then elaborate on the most significant or impactful difference. | *My work offers a novel perspective in [your field] through [highlight unique aspects or innovations]. This fresh approach is demonstrated by [provide specific examples or comparisons].* |
| **How do your results impact the broader field?** | **Connect your findings to real-world implications.** Briefly state the broader significance of your findings, then provide 1–2 examples of how your research could be applied or how it impacts the field. | *This research offers potential solutions to pressing challenges in [mention specific areas or applications]. This is significant because [explain broader impact].* |
| **What limitations does your study have?** | **Acknowledge limitations, but emphasize their mitigation and overall impact.** Briefly state 1–2 key limitations, then explain how you addressed them or why they don’t significantly impact your overall findings. | *While this study has limitations, such as [mention limitations], it’s important to note that steps were taken to mitigate these, including [explain mitigating strategies]. Therefore, the overall findings remain robust.* |
| **Can you discuss the significance of your chosen theory and why you chose it for your study?** | **Focus on the theoretical framework and its contribution to your research.** Briefly explain how the chosen theory informed your research and analysis, and why it was particularly useful for understanding your topic. | *Unlike other theoretical approaches, this theory uniquely captures the complexities of [phenomenon under investigation]. This allows for a nuanced and insightful analysis.* |
| **How did you ensure the reliability and validity of data?** | **Be specific about the measures you took.** Briefly mention 1–2 key strategies you used to ensure reliability and validity, then elaborate on the most important measure. | *Following established best practices, I ensured reliability through techniques like data triangulation and inter-rater reliability. Similarly, validity was prioritized using accepted measures such as [explain measures like content validity, face validity, etc.].* |
| **How would you respond to criticism of your methodology?** | **Acknowledge potential weaknesses, but defend your choices.** Briefly acknowledge 1 potential criticism of your methodology, then explain why you believe your approach was still justified and appropriate. Show humility without undermining your work. | *The methodology, though not without limitations, was selected for its particular ability to address my research question. Its strengths [explain] provide a strong foundation for my findings. Nevertheless, I recognize the potential value of alternative methodological approaches.* |
| **What future research do you recommend based on your findings?** | **Suggest future directions, considering both limitations and promising areas.** Briefly mention 1–2 specific recommendations for future research, building on your findings and addressing potential limitations of your study. | *Urgent exploration of [suggest potential areas for further investigation] is needed to address the knowledge gaps identified in my findings. This research is essential for developing a more robust understanding of [your topic].* |
| **How did you handle ethical considerations in your research?** | **Demonstrate your commitment to ethical research.** Briefly state 1–2 key ethical considerations in your research, then mention the specific steps you took to address them. | *From the outset, ethical considerations were paramount in my research design. This included obtaining informed consent, rigorously protecting confidentiality, and [mention other measures]. Potential ethical concerns were carefully addressed, always adhering to [relevant ethical guidelines].* |
| **How do your conclusions align with your initial research questions?** | **Restate your questions and connect them to your findings.** Briefly revisit your initial research questions, then summarize how your conclusions answer each question. | *My conclusions provide clear answers to the initial research questions: [revisit questions]. This demonstrates that [summarize key findings].* |
| **What is the practical impact of your research?** | **Focus on real-world applications and potential impact.** Briefly explain 1–2 specific practical applications of your research, then mention the potential impact or benefit they could have. Be specific and concrete. | *My research tackles real-world problems by offering practical insights for [mention specific practical applications]. Applying these findings could lead to advancements in [describe potential real-world impacts].* |
