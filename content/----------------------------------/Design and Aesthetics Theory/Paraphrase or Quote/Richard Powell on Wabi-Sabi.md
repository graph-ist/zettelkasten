[[Wabi-Sabi]] cultivates all that is authentic by recognizing three simple realities: nothing lasts, nothing is finished, and nothing is perfect. — [[Richard Powell]] in *Wabi-Sabi Simple* [^1].

[^1]: Powell, R. R. (2005). _Wabi sabi simple: Create beauty, value imperfection, live deeply_. Adams Media.
