[[David Dewing]], director of the Geffrye Museum in [[London]] and a former furniture designer trained at Ravensbourne, has expressed concern for the future of British [[Design]].

“You are looking for an education that is more computer-based and less about hands-on learning,” he notes. “The worry is that if you disconnect the [[Design]] process too much from the process of making, you end up with lower-quality products.”
