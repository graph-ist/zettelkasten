[[Andrea Branzi]] observes that all historical [[Design]] schools began as anti-schools, because in [[E1 - Modernity and Contemporaneity in the 20th Century|modernity]] there is a contradiction between spontaneity and learning. The [[Ulm School of Design]] was the exception: in a particular moment of European history, it proposed a completely utopian and surreal alliance between democracy, science, and [[E1 - Modernity and Contemporaneity in the 20th Century|modernity]], offering [[Europe]] a vision of a trustworthy [[E1 - Modernity and Contemporaneity in the 20th Century|modernity]].
