The carpet is no longer the three-dimensional, predictable Euclidean space of classical physics, homogeneous, isotropic, and measurable in terms of length or distance, but rather a symbolic space. It can even be a non-place, a transit station, a border crossing, or a site of identity loss, cf. [[Henry Corbin, philosopher of the imaginal]].
