[[Philosophy of science by John Sanders]] by [[Sanders]] (Rochester Institute of Technology).  
Lectures exploring the logic, structure, and epistemology of scientific inquiry. Sanders emphasizes how science differs from other forms of knowledge through its reliance on empirical testing, falsifiability ([[Popper]]), and the theory-ladenness of observation ([[Kuhn]], [[Norwood [[Russell]] Hanson]]).  

Connects to [[Design Epistemology]]: both science and [[Design|design]] rely on iterative testing, model-building, and situated reasoning.  
Also relates to [[Luhmann and systems theory]] — seeing science as a self-correcting system of beliefs guided by evidence and methodological skepticism.
