This knowledge base documents my research across philosophy, cognitive science, and computer science.

Following [[Luhmann]]'s [[Zettelkasten]] method, notes function as a networked thinking tool: each connects to others through explicit links, enabling emergent conceptual structures. The system combines atomic notes (discrete concepts) with synthesis notes (broader theoretical domains). The Graph View reveals thematic connections without imposing hierarchy.

Some notes were developed with LLM assistance and may contain errors—they serve as starting points for study, not definitive sources.
