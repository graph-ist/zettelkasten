This research archive integrates philosophy, cognitive science, and computer science through [[Luhmann]]'s [[Zettelkasten]] method. Notes function as interconnected epistemic units: each establishes explicit links with others, generating emergent conceptual structures without imposed hierarchy.

The system distinguishes atomic notes (discrete concepts) from synthesis notes (theoretical domains). The Graph View reveals thematic patterns through connection topology, transforming the archive into a networked thinking instrument.

Some notes were developed with LLM assistance and may contain errors—they serve as starting points for study, not definitive sources.
