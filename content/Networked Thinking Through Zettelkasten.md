Luhmann's approach treats the Zettelkasten (note box) not just as a repository but as a dynamic thinking tool. Each note is linked to others, forming a **networked system** where ideas develop via connections. The system thrives on **atomic notes** (one idea per note), **bidirectional links**, and maintaining a **folgezettel** (sequence note) structure.
