#### Abstract

The study of René Descartes' life and works offers invaluable insights into the development of modern Western philosophy, addressing the problem of knowledge and reality. By examining Descartes' intellectual biography, his approach to mathematics, natural philosophy, and metaphysics, this research sheds light on the historical context that shaped his thoughts. The main findings highlight Descartes' innovative contributions to various fields, including his method of doubt and the concept of Cartesian coordinates. The implications of this study are profound, as it demonstrates the significance of Descartes' ideas in understanding the mind-body problem, the nature of reality, and the foundations of knowledge. Furthermore, it underscores the enduring relevance of Descartes' philosophical legacy in contemporary debates, making his work a crucial component of ongoing academic discourse.
